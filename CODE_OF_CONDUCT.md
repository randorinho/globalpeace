# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct for every project space — the repository (issues, pull
requests, reviews), and any project-run discussion channel.

**In short:** participation here must be harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation. We act and
interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive,
and healthy community — a platform for peace starts by practicing it.

**Enforcement & reporting:** report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer
at randorinho@mbuintelligence.com. All reports are reviewed within 7 days and
handled with discretion; the maintainer may warn, restrict, or ban
contributors who violate this code, following the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines. Reporters' privacy is protected.

**Minors:** the project's public platform has no accounts and no
user-to-user interaction. Project contribution spaces (GitHub) are governed
by GitHub's own age and safety policies in addition to this code.
