# Funding Model — Public & Non-Profit

Investigated 2026-10-12. The platform currently has no external financing.
This is the model for changing that without compromising what makes it
fundable: independence, open methodology, and auditable governance.

## Funding principles (non-negotiable, part of our grant pitch)

1. **Independence**: no single funder above 30% of annual budget once past
   seed stage; funding never buys influence over verification, criteria, or
   the HRA's published positions (docs/GOVERNANCE.md requires a public
   funding policy — this is it).
2. **Exclusions**: no funding from parties to conflicts we track, arms
   producers/brokers, or any source conditioning editorial outcomes.
3. **Radical transparency**: every grant and donation published (amount,
   source, purpose) on a public funding page; annual external audit.
4. **Public-good lock**: the platform remains open source; funding builds
   the commons, never a proprietary fork.

Our existing assets ARE the pitch: live platform, open public repo,
CI-gated releases, AI-Act-oriented compliance (docs/COMPLIANCE.md),
an independent Human Rights Agent auditing the platform itself, and
append-only logs external authorities can inspect. Few applicants at this
stage can show that.

## What we ask money FOR (grant-ready work packages)

1. **Operational base** (P0): hosting, backups, monitoring, security audits.
2. **Verification depth** (P1): ACLED/UNHCR integrations, geolocation,
   multilingual coverage — directly fundable as "early-warning data commons".
3. **Solutions Engine** (P2): briefing + dispatch + outcome tracking — the
   peace-impact story funders want to measure.
4. **Governance** (P3): scientific/peace councils, external audits,
   methodology publication — fundable as institutional strengthening.
