# Governance, Ethics, and Security

## Core safety rule

> If information could endanger a person, the platform must not publish it automatically — even when the information is accurate.

## Security and privacy requirements

- Encryption in transit and at rest
- Multifactor authentication
- Separation between public and confidential data
- Personal-data minimisation
- Pseudonymisation of testimonies
- Automatic redaction of names and sensitive locations
- Role-based access control
- Immutable audit logs
- Protection against scraping and denial-of-service attacks
- Risk assessment before publishing exact locations
- Ability to remove or hide information that could endanger people

The platform creates significant risks involving surveillance, propaganda, defamation, and exposure of informants — these controls are not optional.

## Public participation and learning

People can contribute, but the system never learns directly from public comments. Workflow:

```
Public contribution
    ↓
Spam, abuse, and personal-data detection
    ↓
Classification as idea, testimony, or claim
    ↓
Verification against independent sources
    ↓
Review by local moderators
    ↓
Expert assessment
    ↓
Incorporation as evidence, rule, or proposal
```

Learning is controlled through:

- A versioned knowledge base
- Logs showing who approved each change
- Multilingual evaluation datasets
- Tests against coordinated manipulation
- A public correction mechanism
- Separation between training data and operational data
- **No direct self-learning in production**

Local actors have a central role: they often understand causes, relationships, and solutions that do not appear in international sources.

## Institutional model

To gain legitimacy, establish an independent structure with:

- A scientific council
- A peace council with regional representatives
- International-law experts
- Human-rights and cybersecurity specialists
- Local organisations
- Journalists and fact-checkers
- Representatives from affected communities
- A public funding policy
- An annual external audit

The project must be independent of geopolitical blocs, publish the same methodology for all countries, and disclose conflicts of interest.

## Mission statement

> To build a global, open, and responsible peace-intelligence infrastructure that transforms verified information into understanding, dialogue, and preventive action, while protecting communities and keeping critical decisions under human responsibility.
